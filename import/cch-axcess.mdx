CCH Axcess exports whichever grid you are looking at. You select every row, click **Export Grid**, and get a CSV — which is exactly what Pernee wants.

<Note>
  Two grids will do this, and either is fine. **Client Manager** is the natural one if you think of your book as a list of clients. **Return Manager** works identically and is the better choice if you would rather export by return.
</Note>

## Export the grid

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Client Manager">
    Sign in to CCH Axcess and open **Client Manager** (or **Return Manager**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show every client">
    In the **Quick Search** criteria, set the filters to **All**, then click **Go**.

    Take a moment here — whatever the grid shows is what gets exported. If you only want one office or one partner's clients, filter to them now rather than trimming the file afterwards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select all rows">
    Click into the grid and press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>A</kbd> to select every client ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add any columns you want to bring across">
    **Export Grid** writes out the grid as you see it, so a column that is not displayed is not exported. If entity type, return form or tax year are not currently columns in your grid, add them before exporting and you will have far less to correct in Pernee's review table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export Grid">
    Open the **Home** tab and click **Export Grid**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the file">
    Choose where to save it, give it a name, and click **Save**. Your client list downloads as a **CSV**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Then bring it into Pernee

Drop the CSV into the card in Getting started, or open **Clients** and choose **Import clients**.

Pernee proposes a legal name, entity type, return form and tax year for every row it found, and shows you all of them in an editable table. **Nothing is written to your book until you press Import.**

<Warning>
  CCH client IDs are worth keeping in the file. Pernee stores them as an external reference, which is what lets it match a client back to your CCH record later instead of creating a duplicate.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Exporting the whole book at once is fine — there is no row limit, and reviewing a long list is quicker than it looks because you are scanning for flagged rows rather than reading every one.
</Tip>

## If the menu looks different

CCH Axcess changes its layout between releases, and firms configure the ribbon differently. If you cannot find **Export Grid** on the **Home** tab, the thing to look for is any *export the grid* command on the grid you are viewing — the resulting CSV is the same. Wolters Kluwer's own support site carries the current steps for your release.

Still stuck? Any file with one row per client works, including a workbook someone keeps by hand. See [Bring your book across](/import/overview).
