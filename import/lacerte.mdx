Lacerte lets you choose exactly which fields leave the program, so this export takes a minute longer than the others and produces a better file for it.

## Export to file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your clients">
    Go to the **Clients** tab and highlight the clients you want to export.

    To select many at once, press <kbd>F3</kbd> to open the **Client Group Selection** window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the export">
    From the **Client** menu, choose **Export**, then **Export to File**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it lands">
    Click **Browse** to pick a folder, or accept the default:

    ```text
    C:\LACERTE\YYTAX\IDATA\EXPORT
    ```

    `YY` is the tax year — `24TAX` for 2024, and so on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the file">
    Enter a filename or keep the default `EXPORT`.

    Avoid these characters, which Lacerte rejects: `/ ? * : < > " |`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the export type to Comma Delimited">
    This is the setting that produces a `.CSV`. Leave it as **Comma Delimited**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the fields">
    Click the plus sign `(+)` beside a category in the **Available** list to expand it, then double-click an item — or highlight it and click `(>)` — to move it into **Display**. Use `(<)` to remove one, and the **Up** / **Down** buttons to reorder.

    Worth including: client name, entity type or return type, tax year, client number, and contact email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the file">
    Click **OK**, then **Exit** once it finishes. Your file is `EXPORT.CSV` in the folder you chose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Don't open it in Excel and re-save it.** Excel strips the leading zeros from ZIP codes, and a re-saved file carries the damage. Send Pernee the CSV Lacerte produced.

  If you do need to open it, format the ZIP column as **Special** first.
</Warning>

## The other way: a Client Listing Report

If you would rather build a report than pick fields in the export dialog, Lacerte's **Client Listing Report** (often called CLIST) does the same job:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the report">
    Open the Client Listing Report and choose the fields you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print to file">
    In the **Report Display** options, choose **Print to file**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the CSV format">
    For file type, select **File (Comma Delimited)**, the `.csv` option.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Lacerte offers to filter the client list before it runs, which is useful if you only want a segment of the book rather than all of it.

## Then bring it into Pernee

Drop the CSV into the card in Getting started, or open **Clients** and choose **Import clients**.

Pernee proposes a legal name, entity type, return form and tax year for every row it found, and shows you all of them in an editable table. **Nothing is written to your book until you press Import.**

<Tip>
  Keeping the Lacerte client number in the export is worth it — Pernee stores it as an external reference, which is what stops a second import creating duplicates.
</Tip>
