SmartVault exports your client list from the web portal. The export runs in the background and arrives by email rather than downloading straight away.

## Export to CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the SmartVault portal">
    Use the web portal, not the Connected Desktop — the client-list export lives in the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Client Management">
    Select **Client Management** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export to CSV">
    Click **Actions** next to **Add Client**, then choose **Export to CSV**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the email">
    SmartVault prepares the file and emails you a link when it is ready. Download the CSV from that link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Want to know who works on what as well? **Manage Employees** in the left sidebar has **Actions** → **Export Assigned Clients**, which exports client assignments per employee. That is useful context for setting up client assignments in Pernee later, but it is not needed for the import itself.
</Note>

## A note on what SmartVault's export contains

SmartVault is a document portal rather than tax software, so its client export is contact-shaped: names, email addresses, and the account structure. It generally will **not** carry entity type, return form or tax year.

That is fine, and Pernee will not invent them. Those fields simply arrive empty in the review table and you set them there — or you import from your tax software instead and use SmartVault only for the documents.

<Tip>
  If you run both SmartVault and one of [CCH Axcess](/import/cch-axcess), [Lacerte](/import/lacerte) or [UltraTax CS](/import/ultratax-cs), export the book from the tax software. It knows the entity types, which saves you filling them in by hand.
</Tip>

## Then bring it into Pernee

Drop the CSV into the card in Getting started, or open **Clients** and choose **Import clients**.

Pernee proposes a legal name, entity type, return form and tax year for every row it found, and shows you all of them in an editable table. **Nothing is written to your book until you press Import.**

## Exporting the documents too

The steps above export the client *list*. If you are moving the files as well, the Connected Desktop can bulk-export them: **File** → **Export data**, choose to include all clients, then pick a folder on your computer.

You usually do not need to. Pernee reads documents from the systems you connect rather than requiring an upload — see [Connect your systems](/getting-started/connect-systems).

Using something else as well? See [Bring your book across](/import/overview).
